## 🏛️ THE COUNCIL DOSSIER — the fusion design study (10-17 overnight, owner-ordered)

### What the science says (researched 10-17)

- **Static combination loses to conditional gating.** Fixed weights and hard AND-gates are the weakest way to combine experts; a gate that decides *per situation* which expert to trust (mixture-of-experts, Jacobs 1991 → modern surveys) consistently wins — and gating on each expert's running confidence/performance is a proven gate design.
- **Every gate is a coverage–risk trade.** (Chow 1970; selective prediction.) Coverage = how often we act; selective risk = how wrong the acted moments are. Our machine sits at ~0.2% coverage with near-zero risk — the extreme corner. The honest question is never "open the doors" but **"how much coverage can we buy back per unit of risk"** — answered only by sweeping thresholds on recorded days.
- **A gate earns its keep only if the risk it removes is large relative to the coverage it consumes — and thresholds calibrated on one evidence regime (lake days) lie on another (river days).** So any new floor must be graded per regime, on multiple days, before it goes live.
